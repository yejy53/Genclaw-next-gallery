🌍 What is Minecraft?

Minecraft is a game made of blocks! You can dig, build, explore, and survive in a world you create yourself. Every tree, mountain, and cave is made of cube-shaped blocks that you can break and place anywhere you like.


💡 Fun Fact: Minecraft worlds are HUGE — almost 8 times bigger than the planet Earth!

Two ways to play:

Survival Mode

Gather resources, fight monsters, and stay alive. Watch your health and hunger!

Creative Mode

Fly around with unlimited blocks and build anything you can imagine!

🧱 Famous Blocks

Blocks are the building bricks of everything. Here are some of the most important ones:


Grass Block

The green block you start on. Dirt with grass on top!

Stone

Found underground. Mine it to make tools and buildings.

Wood / Logs

Chop trees to get wood — your very first crafting material.

Diamond Ore

Sparkly and super rare! Makes the strongest tools.

Gold Ore

Shiny but soft. Great for trading and fancy gear.

Emerald

The money of Minecraft! Villagers love to trade for these.

Obsidian

Super tough purple-black block. Used to build Nether portals.

TNT

KABOOM! Light it up and it explodes. Handle with care!

🐾 Creatures & Mobs

“Mobs” are the living creatures in Minecraft. Some are friendly, some want to give you a scare!


🙁

Creeper Hostile

The most famous monster! It sneaks up quietly and goes Sssss… BOOM! Watch your back.

🧟

Zombie Hostile

Comes out at night and shuffles toward you. They burn up in the morning sun!

👤

Enderman Neutral

Tall and spooky. It can teleport and pick up blocks. Don’t stare into its eyes!

🐄

Cow Friendly

Gives you milk and food. Totally harmless and very friendly.

🚪 The Three Dimensions

The Overworld

Home base! Forests, oceans, mountains, and villages. This is where you begin.

The Nether

A fiery underground world full of lava and tricky monsters. Reach it through a portal.

The End

A dark floating island where the mighty Ender Dragon lives. The final boss!


🐉 Beating the Ender Dragon is the closest thing to “winning” Minecraft!

⭐ Top Tips for New Players

  • Chop wood first — you need it to craft everything else.
  • Build a shelter before your first night. Monsters come out in the dark!
  • Place torches everywhere. Light keeps monsters from spawning.
  • Never dig straight down — you might fall into lava!
  • Keep a bed nearby. Sleeping skips the scary night.
  • Always carry food so your hunger bar stays full.
