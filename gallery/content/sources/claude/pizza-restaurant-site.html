How We Cook Your Pizza

Four unhurried steps between the flour and your first bite.

🌾

Slow Dough

Our dough rests and ferments for 48 hours, developing a light, airy crust with real depth of flavor.

🍅

Fresh Toppings

Hand-crushed San Marzano tomatoes, fresh mozzarella, and garden basil laid on by hand each morning.

🔥

Wood-Fired

90 blazing seconds in our 450°C brick oven give the crust its signature leopard-spotted char.

🍕

Served Hot

Sliced, boxed, and rushed to your table or door while the cheese is still gloriously molten.

Since 1998

The heart of it all is our brick oven

Built by hand from Neapolitan clay and fired only with seasoned oak, our oven has never once gone cold in twenty-five years.

That living fire is what turns simple flour, water, and tomato into something worth crossing town for. We wouldn't cook a pizza any other way.
